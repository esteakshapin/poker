Chip counts by session

Enter each player's chip stack at the end of a session. Leave blank if they didn't play. A filled-in cell counts as a session played. The check rows compare what was counted with what should be on the table: everyone's stack coming in, plus that session's buy-ins. Chips that go missing are lost and their value goes to the organization. More chips than expected, or a buy-in with no count, is flagged as an error.

Per-player summary

Chips can't be cashed out, so money only changes hands at the end: the top 2 qualified players get their share of the prize pool and everyone else's buy-ins stay in the pot. "Money result" is what each player would win minus what they paid in, if the tournament ended now.
